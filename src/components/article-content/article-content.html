How to renew your business permit online

Quezon City residents and business owners can now renew business permits entirely online through the QCitizen portal. The process takes less than ten minutes and eliminates the need to visit the city hall.

What you need to prepare

Before starting your application, prepare your tax clearance, the current year's business permit, and your lease contract if you are renting your business space. Make sure these documents are scanned and clear before uploading.

Step-by-step guide

Log in to the portal, select the renewal option, and follow the prompts to upload your documents. The system will compute the applicable fees automatically. Pay using any of the supported e-wallets and you will receive your digital permit by email within three working days.
