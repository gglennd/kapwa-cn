Biography

Mayor Jane Doe was elected in 2022 on a platform of digital governance and transparent service delivery. Under her leadership, the city launched over forty online services and cut average processing times for permits by half.

A former economist, she previously served as the city's budget director where she championed open data. She believes government information belongs to the public and has pushed for every city agency to publish its data by default.
