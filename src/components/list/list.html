Business permit renewal

Permits
Apply online

Renew your business permit entirely online. Attach your tax clearance, lease contract, and the previous permit. Pay the assessed fees using GCash, PayMaya, or any partner bank. Once approved, your digital permit is emailed to you within three working days.

Permits

Register a new business with the city. A barangay clearance, community tax certificate, and a valid government ID are required before submission.

Senior citizen ID application

Welfare
Apply online

Apply for a senior citizen ID and OSCA certificate. Submit a scanned copy of your PSA birth certificate, one 1x1 photo, and proof of Quezon City residency. The ID is free of charge and can be claimed at the city hall or delivered to your address if you register for doorstep delivery.
